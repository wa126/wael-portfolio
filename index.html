About me

I am a Data Analyst passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions. I specialize in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

Through practical projects involving sales performance, pizza sales, coffee shop operations, and football player performance, I have developed hands-on experience in organizing datasets, identifying trends, analyzing business performance, and presenting findings through clear and interactive visualizations.

My workflow combines data preparation, analysis, data modeling, calculations, and visualization to turn complex datasets into actionable insights. I focus on creating dashboards that are not only visually appealing but also useful, intuitive, and aligned with business needs.

I am continuously improving my analytical and technical skills, with a particular focus on Power BI, SQL, business intelligence, and data-driven problem-solving. My goal is to help businesses understand their data, monitor performance, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions.
